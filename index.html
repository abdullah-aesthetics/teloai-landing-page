The Problem

Most practices don't have a lead problem. They have a response problem.

Your marketing may be working. The problem is what happens after the enquiry comes in.

Leads arrive outside working hours

Patients enquire in the evenings, weekends and during busy clinic hours. By the time your team replies, they've often booked elsewhere.

Marketing spend gets wasted

Meta, Google, SEO and open days all drive interest — but delayed responses mean practices lose opportunities they've already paid to generate.

Dead leads stay dead

Most practices have hundreds of old enquiries, unfinished conversations and patients who never booked. That database is worth thousands.
